# Botanical surfaces and endgrain

[Source contact sheet](botanical-and-endgrain-v1.png) created with the built-in ImageGen tool for EZ Environment. The app ships cropped, tint-adjusted albedo files and matching normal and roughness maps derived from the artwork.

## ImageGen prompt

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Create a single square texture-source contact sheet of four different macro surface samples for the EZ Environment 3D authoring app. Strict edge-to-edge 2×2 grid with the dividing lines exactly at the middle, no gutters, no captions. Each quadrant is a full square of texture filled right up to its edges. Top left: flat transverse cut of real weathered pale warm-grey hardwood log end grain, center of the growth rings near the quadrant's exact center, closely spaced organic concentric rings, tiny pores and a few subtle radial checks. No outside bark, outline or background. Top right: outer green plant stem skin seen extremely close, longitudinal fine ribbing, occasional slender fibers, matte waxy cuticle, no nodes, very even distribution. Bottom left: a pale ivory flower petal surface with delicate lengthwise veins and velvety fine cells, very subtle cross-grain. Bottom right: close-up sunflower disk pollen microtexture with very fine densely packed round granules, matte, evenly spread. All four top-down orthographic, flat even diffuse illumination, neutral contrast and no directional highlights, gradients, cast shadows, perspective, labels, boundaries within each tile, watermarks or objects. The three botanical swatches should read as continuous evenly detailed materials suitable for repeating; use restrained natural colors suitable for material tinting.
