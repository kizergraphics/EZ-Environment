# Tree species textures

The eight new bark and foliage sets use original artwork generated with the built-in ImageGen tool. Each species folder contains bark and foliage base color, normal, and roughness maps. The texture preparation step crops the four atlases into individual materials, blends opposing bark edges for repeat wrapping, keeps the foliage alpha, and derives the detail maps from local image contrast.

## ImageGen prompts

**Broadleaf bark**

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: four bark albedo texture swatches for an offline tree modeling app. Generate one square atlas in an exact 2 columns × 2 rows edge-to-edge grid. The boundaries are at exactly half the image. Each quadrant is one independently continuous evenly lit front-facing macro bark photo texture with fine details across the entire quadrant. Top left: paper birch, chalk-white horizontal papery peeling layers, thin charcoal lenticels. Top right: weeping willow, dark warm olive gray-brown, long sinuous deeply furrowed ridges. Bottom left: sugar maple, mature warm gray overlapping chunky scaly plates and shallow irregular cracks. Bottom right: ginkgo, muted khaki gray bark with narrow corky braided vertical ridges. Render true-to-surface microtexture without cast shadows, highlights, branches, moss, lichen, hands, objects, gradients, labels, borders or gutters. All bark grain aligned straight vertical except horizontal birch lenticels. Treat each quadrant as a flat material capture. Maximize fine grain and natural tonal variation.

**Conifer and palm bark**

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: four bark albedo texture swatches for an offline tree modeling app. Generate one square atlas in an exact 2 columns × 2 rows edge-to-edge grid. The boundaries are at exactly half the image. Each quadrant is one independently continuous evenly lit front-facing macro bark photo texture with fine details across the entire quadrant. Top left: Colorado blue spruce gray-brown bark, dense small rounded thin flaking scales with fine rust-gray crevices. Top right: Coast Redwood, saturated copper cinnamon bark, strongly vertical shredded stringy fibers and soft dark furrows. Bottom left: Lebanon Cedar, charcoal and sepia bark, rugged blocky square plates with deep angular gray fissures. Bottom right: Mediterranean Fan Palm stem, closely stacked horizontal brown-gray ribbed rings and woven tawny leaf-base scars. Render true-to-surface microtexture without cast shadows, highlights, branches, moss, lichen, hands, objects, gradients, labels, borders, or gutters. Treat each quadrant as a flat material capture. Maximize fine grain and natural tonal variation.

**Broadleaf foliage**

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: square RGBA foliage sprite atlas for alpha-tested 3D tree cards. Generate an exact 2×2 contact sheet of four separate botanical cutouts on genuinely transparent background, one centered in each quadrant with generous transparent gutters so the sprites never touch the quadrant boundaries. Top left: a small paper-birch green twig spray of 5 pointed ovate serrated leaves, clear triangular tapered shapes and light lime green tones. Top right: a hanging narrow V-shaped spray of weeping willow, many long drooping narrow lance-shaped gray-green leaves on thin twigs, high-botanical accuracy and deep open gaps. Bottom left: sugar maple cluster of 3 differently sized distinct five-pointed bright golden-orange autumn leaves (classic Canadian maple shape), with clear lobes and veins. Bottom right: a spray of several golden yellow ginkgo leaves with broad rounded fluted fan shapes, clear center notches and fine radiating veins, each attached to a short visible petiole. Overall outline of every cutout foliage spray should extend primarily upwards or outwards from a short stem emerging at the bottom center, enough open gaps that the alpha cutout remains delicate. Front-on top-down specimen, soft diffuse light, sharp edges, true color. No shadows, border, tile background, text, labels, or any neighboring sprites overlapping.

**Conifer and palm foliage**

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: square RGBA foliage sprite atlas for alpha-tested 3D tree cards. Generate an exact 2×2 contact sheet of four separate botanical cutouts on genuinely transparent background, one centered in each quadrant with generous transparent gutters so the sprites never touch the quadrant boundaries. Top left: blue spruce twig sprig, icy silvery blue and sage-blue pointed radial needles, distinct dense bottlebrush tips on a V-shaped branch. Top right: Coast Redwood fern-like flat spray with rich dark green narrow linear paired needles on slender reddish green branchlets, distinct feather pattern, open gaps between branchlets. Bottom left: Lebanon Cedar branchlet arranged like a horizontal layered spray of small green needle rosettes on airy arching cedar twigs; longer darker olive needles radiate in individual star-shaped bunches. Bottom right: one broad Mediterranean Fan Palm frond, strongly pleated emerald green deeply separated fingerlike ray segments fanning outward, oval fan silhouette and short stem at bottom center. All four must be separated static top-down cutouts with stems at bottom center, visible negative space between needles or fingers, nearly uniform diffuse flat light, sharp silhouette, natural visible veins. No shadows, border, tile background, text, labels, or overlap.
