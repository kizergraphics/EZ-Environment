# Grass card textures

Each of the nine types has 12 individually generated foliage variants and a seed-head tile. The artwork was made with the built-in ImageGen tool. The first three rows of each packed PNG contain the foliage variants. The bottom-left tile contains the seed-head variant. Meadow Grass reuses its original meadow seed-head artwork.

The images were checked at the editor’s alpha cutoff and against a contrasting background. Packing preserves the generated alpha and adds color padding around the blades for texture filtering.

True transparent RGBA alpha behind and inside each clump. Leave 8% fully transparent padding on EVERY side of EVERY cell, keeping all blades far from the 4-by-3 grid boundaries. Each sprite has a narrow root crown at its own lower center and has a separate transparent gutter. Subject for all twelve sprites: Fine Fescue. Realistic cool olive to vivid green fine bunchgrass, wispy hair-thin blades and loose fountain arches, airy open centers and irregular low grassy tussocks. Distinct spread and loops. No seed heads. All twelve are the same botanical species, each an independently photographed clump with a DIFFERENT detailed silhouette. Vary sparse vs full, bent left vs right, narrow vs fan-shaped, and short vs tall. Especially emphasize visible open TRANSPARENT gaps between individual blades; very loose airy natural structure. Eye-level side/front, no overhead. Soft even diffuse lighting, crisp detailed silhouettes, neutral photographic texture. No soil, shadow, ground plane, blur, dark/colored halo, background color, fog, border, labels, merged specimens or printed words.
